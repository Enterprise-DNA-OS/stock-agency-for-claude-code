# Record checks and their sources

`npm run agency -- compliance` (the `/compliance` command) checks the records against the rules below. Each check is a query in the `v_compliance` view in `supabase/migrations/0001_stock_agency.sql`. A clean result means the records are complete and consistent with the rule as written here. It does not mean the agency is compliant: the transfer itself is done in the NLIS or NAIT database, the levy return is lodged with the Department of Agriculture, and both are your duty.

Sources were read on 12 November 2026. Rules change: the cattle transaction levy is under review in 2026, and states write their own NLIS rules. If a source says something different from this page, the source wins: update this page and the check together (`/customise`).

| Rule | What the data must show | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `NLIS-TRANSFER-2-DAYS` | Every sold lot in Australia has its NLIS transfer recorded within `transfer_days` (default 2) of the sale. In NSW an agent must move stock off their agent code to the destination PIC within 2 days, and for online sales the transfer must also be done within 2 days. A transfer done late stays on the record as breached. Other states set their own timing: change `transfer_days` with `set settings --transfer-days=N` and cite your state's rule here. | [NSW DPI: NLIS for stock agents, quick guide](https://www.dpi.nsw.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0007/1468420/National-Livestock-Identification-System-Quick-Guide-NLIS-for-stock-agents.pdf) |
| `NAIT-48-HOURS` | In New Zealand, a movement between NAIT locations that is not through an accredited saleyard (a private, paddock, online or clearing sale) is recorded within 48 hours of the end of the day the animals moved. Accredited saleyards and meat processors record their own movements, so saleyard sales are left out of the check. | [OSPRI: Recording animal movements](https://www.ospri.co.nz/farmers-and-livestock-owners/moving-animals/recording-animal-movements) |
| `NVD-ON-FILE` | Every sold lot has its vendor declaration number recorded. The agent must keep a copy of the NVD and be able to produce it. The NSW agent guide says at least 2 years; NSW DPI lists the LPA NVD as a 7-year record for producers. Keep yours for 7. History imported from AgriNous is not checked: its declarations stay in AgriNous. | [NSW DPI: NLIS for stock agents](https://www.dpi.nsw.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0007/1468420/National-Livestock-Identification-System-Quick-Guide-NLIS-for-stock-agents.pdf), [NSW DPI: NLIS online sales](https://www.dpi.nsw.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0007/1469833/Prime-fact-NLIS-Online-sales.pdf) |
| `NVD-TO-ABATTOIR` | Stock sold to slaughter has its NVD (or a copy) sent to the abattoir before delivery or with the stock: recorded as sent on or before the sale day. | [NSW DPI: NLIS for stock agents](https://www.dpi.nsw.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0007/1468420/National-Livestock-Identification-System-Quick-Guide-NLIS-for-stock-agents.pdf) |
| `NVD-TO-BUYER-7-DAYS` | For every other movement, the selling agent gives the buyer a copy of the NVD within `nvd_to_buyer_days` (default 7) of the sale. | [NSW DPI: NLIS for stock agents](https://www.dpi.nsw.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0007/1468420/National-Livestock-Identification-System-Quick-Guide-NLIS-for-stock-agents.pdf) |
| `TRANSACTION-LEVY` | Every sold cattle, sheep and lamb lot on an account sale written here carries its transaction levy, or is below the price the levy starts at. The rates in the demo `charge_rates`: cattle $5.00 a head (bobby calves 90 cents, not modelled: add a rate if you sell them); lambs 2% of the sale price, no more than $1.50 a head; sheep 2%, no more than 20 cents a head; no levy on sheep or lambs sold under $5 a head. The levies carry no GST. Point-of-sale agents collect them and lodge a monthly or annual return. | [Department of Agriculture: cattle transaction levy](https://www.agriculture.gov.au/agriculture-land/farm-food-drought/levies/rates/cattle-livestock-transaction), [Department of Agriculture: sheep and lamb transaction levy](https://www.agriculture.gov.au/agriculture-land/farm-food-drought/levies/rates/sheep-and-lamb-transaction) |
| `LEVY-RETURN` | Every past month with levies held has a return recorded as lodged (`/lodge-levy`). The check shows a month as due, not breached: the due date depends on whether you lodge monthly or annually, so check it on your return. | [Department of Agriculture: cattle transaction levy](https://www.agriculture.gov.au/agriculture-land/farm-food-drought/levies/rates/cattle-livestock-transaction) |
| `VENDOR-SETTLEMENT` | Every account sale is paid to the vendor by settlement day: `vendor_settle_days` (default 7) after the sale. | Agency policy. If your state or your trust account rules set a time, cite it here. |

## What the checks do not do

- They do not record a transfer in the NLIS or NAIT database, lodge a levy return, or pay anyone. They read your records and say what is missing.
- They do not check trust account rules, licence conditions, animal welfare in transport, LPA accreditation, PigPass or the national residue survey. Add a rule with `/customise` when your agency needs one, with its source.
- They read what was entered. A sale run in another system and never imported is invisible to them.
